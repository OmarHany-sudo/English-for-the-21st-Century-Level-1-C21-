[revision-p16-1] 103. راجع الفاعل أو ااًل، وبعدها اختار الفعل أو الضمير أو أداة الملكية.

[revision-p16-2] 
Rule | Use | Structure | Example | Exam note
Verb to be | تعريف/وصف/بلد/ عمر | I am; he/she/it is; you/we/they are | She is Egyptian. | لا تستخدم do مع سؤال be.
Be – negative | نفي معلومة | be + not | They aren't students. | isn't للمفرد؛ aren't للجمع.
Be – question | سؤال نعم/لا | Am/Is/Are + subject? | Are you ready? | ابدأ بـ be.
Short answers | رد مختصر | Yes, subject + be / No, subject + be + not | Yes, I am. | لا تقل Yes, I do مع be.
Wh- with be | طلب معلومة | Wh + be + subject? | Where is she from? | What's = What is.
Pronouns | بديل الاسم | he/she/it/we/they | Sara = she. | Ali and I = we.
Possessive adjectives | تحديد صاحب الاسم | my/your/his/her/its/our/ their + noun | Her name is Sara. | تأتي قبل الاسم.
Possessive 's | ملكية مفرد | singular + 's + noun | Kamal's sister. | apostrophe قبل s.
Plural possessive | ملكية جمع منتٍبـه s | plural + ' + noun | parents' house. | apostrophe بعد s.
Irregular plural possessive | جمع شاذ | plural + 's | children's school. | children لا تنتهي بـ s.
Have / Has | امتلاك/وجود | I/you/we/they have؛ he/she/it has | Kamal has a sister. | اختار حسب الفاعل.
in / with | وصف مظهر | in + clothes؛ with + hair/features | in a shirt; with glasses | الملابس in، الملامح with.
Present Simple positive | عادة/روتين | I/you/we/they + base verb | They work. | لا تضف s مع they.
Present Simple – he/she/it | عادة/روتين | he/she/it + verb-s/es | She studies. | انتبه لـ ies → y و es.
Present Simple negative | نفي عادة | don't/doesn't + base verb | He doesn't work. | بعد doesn't مصدر.
Present Simple question | سؤال عادة | Do/Does + subject + base verb? | Do you study? | بعد Does مصدر.
a / an | اسم مفرد معدود | a + consonant sound؛ an + vowel sound | a university; an engineer | الصوت وليس الحرف.
